# Code of Conduct

We want survey-agent to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of
background or identity.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive; assume good faith; critique ideas, not people; help
newcomers.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing others' private
information.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
v2.1 as its standard of behavior. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments
that violate it, and may ban repeat or serious offenders.

**Reporting:** email **paul@neto.me** (subject: "survey-agent conduct"). Reports are
handled confidentially and in good faith.
